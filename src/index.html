      🇱🇷 eFootball Liberia

      🏆 Official League Rules — Season 1

      1. Player Eligibility

      Only registered players who have been officially accepted into the league may participate. Each player must provide their correct eFootball name/ID and any other information required by the organizers.

      2. Match Schedule

      Players must complete their assigned fixtures within the announced matchweek deadline. Players are responsible for communicating with their opponents and arranging a suitable time.

      3. Match Results

      After every match, the result must be reported to the league administrators with a screenshot or other required evidence. False or manipulated results will result in disciplinary action.

      4. Points System

      League points will be awarded as follows:

      • Win = 3 points
      • Draw = 1 point
      • Loss = 0 points

      The league table will also record matches played, wins, draws, losses, goals scored, goals conceded and goal difference.

      5. League Ranking

      Players will be ranked primarily by points. If players are tied on points, the following tiebreakers will apply:

      1. Goal difference
      2. Goals scored
      3. Head-to-head result
      4. Organizer's official ruling if still tied

      6. Connection & Disconnection Issues

      Players must have a reasonable and stable internet connection before starting a match. If a match is disconnected, players must immediately provide evidence and contact an administrator. The organizer will determine whether the match should continue, be replayed, or have another outcome.

      7. Fair Play

      Cheating, exploiting game bugs, manipulating results, intentionally disconnecting to avoid a loss, using another person's account, or any other attempt to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.

      8. Respect & Conduct

      Players must respect opponents, administrators and other members. Insults, harassment, threats, discrimination, excessive provocation or disruptive behavior will not be tolerated.

      9. Missed Matches & Forfeits

      Players who repeatedly fail to respond, refuse to play scheduled fixtures, or miss deadlines without a valid reason may receive a forfeit. Repeated inactivity can result in removal from the league.

      10. Final Authority

      The league administrators have the final authority regarding disputes, scheduling, disciplinary matters and interpretation of these rules. Decisions will be based on available evidence and applied as fairly and consistently as possible.

      THE eFOOTBALL LIBERIA STANDARD

      Play Fair. Respect Your Opponent. Compete to Win. 🇱🇷🎮

      Rules may be updated between seasons to improve the competition. Any changes made during a season will be communicated clearly to all participating players.
